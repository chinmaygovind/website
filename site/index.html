whales

In summer of 2025, I had the chance to work in the Penn Undergraduate Research Program under Dr. John Spiesberger in his whales research group. I got to work on novel methods for using machine learning to calculate time differences of arrival of whale calls in order to track their locations. I'm super grateful to Dr. Spiesberger and Ari Spiesberger as well as PURM for giving me my first professional machine learning experience!

drive

ticket to ride

This is a clone I made of the board game "Ticket to Ride" because my friends and I really liked to play it in real life, but we're all thousands of miles apart. You can make a room to play online with friends and bot opponents. Go to ttr.cgovind.com to play!

egyptian rat screw

This is a recreation of the card game Egyptian Rat Screw, a card game involving slapping the table. Visit ers.cgovind.com to play!

king of tokyo

This is my version of King of Tokyo, the dice game where giant monsters fight over Tokyo. Grab friends for a room or fill the table with bots. I first learned this game at a church retreat camp when I was about 13 years old. I loved playing it then, and my friend Krish reminded me of its existence recently and I really wanted to play again, so I had to make an online version. Try kot.cgovind.com to play!

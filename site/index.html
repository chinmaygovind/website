I like to play cards. I may not be the best, but I do get pretty lucky.

  • Understanding Poker: the basics of playing the game

whales

In summer of 2025, I had the chance to work in the Penn Undergraduate Research Program under Dr. John Spiesberger in his whales research group. I got to work on novel methods for using machine learning to calculate time differences of arrival of whale calls in order to track their locations. I'm super grateful to Dr. Spiesberger and Ari Spiesberger as well as PURM for giving me my first professional machine learning experience!

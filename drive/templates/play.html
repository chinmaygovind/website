On a phone

Hold it sideways. Throttle and brake are under your right thumb and steering under your left. There is no separate drift button - there is nowhere a thumb could reach one - so the handbrake is a gesture, and there are two of them. Drag down on the throttle without letting go of it: the slide comes on under power and lets off as you slide back up. Or double-tap and hold the arrow you are turning with, so tap-tap-hold left is a handbrake turn to the left, and letting go of the arrow lets the handbrake go. Whichever you use, the button lights up amber so you can see it took. The flag puts you back at the last checkpoint and the arrow restarts the run.

Getting round quickly

Grass is slow, so a corner cut usually costs more than it saves. Crests throw the car - land flat and you keep the speed. Inside a loop, stay off the brakes: it is your own speed holding you on the wall.

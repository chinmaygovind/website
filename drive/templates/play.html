On a phone

Hold it sideways. Throttle and brake are under your right thumb and steering under your left. There is no separate drift button, because your right thumb never leaves a pedal - instead double-tap the brake and hold and it becomes the handbrake for as long as you keep your thumb down; it lights up amber so you can see it took. The flag puts you back at the last checkpoint and the arrow restarts the run.

Getting round quickly

Grass is slow, so a corner cut usually costs more than it saves. Crests throw the car - land flat and you keep the speed. Inside a loop, stay off the brakes: it is your own speed holding you on the wall.

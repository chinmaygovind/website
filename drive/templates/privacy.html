Privacy

Drive is a hobby project run by one person. It shows no ads, has no trackers on it, and sells nothing to anybody. What it keeps is listed below in full.

If you just play

You can drive every track without an account. For each request your browser makes, the server writes one row recording the time, the page, the referring page, your browser's user agent string, your IP address, and a random id kept in a cookie so that several page views can be recognised as one visit. That is what tells me whether anybody is playing and which tracks they choose.

Lap times you set without an account are kept in your own browser, not on the server, until you make an account. Clearing your browser data removes them.

If you make an account

An account stores a username, an email address, and a hash of your password — never the password itself. Alongside it Drive keeps what you would expect a racing game to keep: your best lap on each track, a recording of the car's movement during those laps so other players can race your ghost, your rating, your medals, and the car you built in the garage. Your username, your times and your ghosts are public, because a leaderboard is the point of them. Your email address is never shown to anyone.

One account works across Drive, Ticket to Ride, Egyptian Rat Screw and King of Tokyo, because they share one accounts system on cgovind.com.

If you play on CrazyGames

Inside CrazyGames the game loads their SDK, which is a script served by them and covered by their privacy policy rather than this one. Drive uses it for two things: telling them when a lap actually starts, which is how they measure loading, and signing you in.

If you are signed in to CrazyGames, an account is made for you here automatically and you never type anything. What is stored is your CrazyGames user id, the name you use there, and a copy of your profile picture — re-encoded and kept on this server, so no page here ever asks their servers for it. There is no password, because there is nothing to log into. Your times then sit on the same public leaderboard as everybody else's.

Your CrazyGames email address is never sent to this game, and Drive never asks you for one in there.

Cookies

Two, both strictly functional, neither used for advertising:

cgv — a random visitor id, so repeat visits can be counted as one person rather than as many. Lasts two years.
session — keeps you logged in, or remembers your guest name. Lasts thirty days.

When Drive is played inside another site that embeds it, both cookies are partitioned: the browser keeps a separate copy for that site, and it cannot be connected to your account here.

What is not here

No advertising, no advertising identifiers, no analytics service, no social network buttons, no data sold or shared with anybody. Nothing is collected beyond what is written above{% if portal %}, and nothing leaves this server except what is needed to check that the CrazyGames sign-in above is genuine{% else %}, and none of it leaves the one server this runs on{% endif %}.

Removing your data

Ask and it is deleted — account, times, ghosts and all. chinmaygov@gmail.com, or the Discord. If you are under 13, please ask a parent or guardian before making an account.

Get the Drive app

Add Drive to your home screen and it opens like any other app — full screen, no address bar, one tap away. It takes about ten seconds and installs nothing.

  1. 1

    Open this page in Safari

    Only Safari can add an app to the iPhone home screen. If you are already in it, carry straight on to step 2.

    Open in Safari
  2. 2

    Tap the Share button

    It sits in the bar along the bottom of Safari.

    Share
  3. 3

    Pick “Add to Home Screen”

    Scroll the list down a little — it is below the row of apps.

    Add to Home Screen
  4. 4

    Tap Add

    Drive lands on your home screen with the rest of your apps.

Your browser can do this for you — no menus needed.

  1. 1

    Open the browser menu

    The three dots in the top right of Chrome.

    Menu
  2. 2

    Tap “Install app”

    Some browsers call it “Add to Home screen” instead.

    Install app
  3. 3

    Confirm

    Drive lands on your home screen with the rest of your apps.

Already added it? Open Drive from the home screen icon and this page will stop asking.

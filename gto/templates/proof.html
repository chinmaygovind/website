What is still not checked

The postflop numbers. Nothing on this page tests them against a solver, because a postflop comparison needs a binary this repository does not ship. That is why every postflop number in a review is labelled model and not solver: it is exact against these five bots, whose strategies are known functions, and it is not a claim about equilibrium. The bet-sizing curve stops at showdown on the street you are on, so it understates a small bet that sets up a bigger one on the next card.
